# White app icons

All installed apps use white SVGs or the original prototype's white branded glyphs. The dock and All apps share the same renderer. No colored Android tile or opaque tile background is shown. New unrecognized packages receive a white monogram until a vector is added to `../icon-map.json`.

Spotify, Proton VPN, Stremio and Google Play paths come from Simple Icons, revision `90646e4bf7f3b89df6c37e65baf3e7bc759a517c`, under CC0. See `LICENSE-SimpleIcons.md`. Other additional icons are simple white vector symbols or wordmarks drawn for this port, including Astra's star. Typeface-based wordmarks use the bundled Manrope font.

Brand names and marks belong to their owners. These are navigation icons for the user's installed apps.
